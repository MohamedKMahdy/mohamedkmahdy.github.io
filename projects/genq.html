Revit · quantity take-off

GenQ

A priced bill of quantities without leaving Revit.

  • C#
  • Revit API 2022 to 2027
  • .NET Framework 4.8 / .NET 8 / .NET 10
  • ClosedXML
  • PDFsharp
  • xUnit

The problem

Quantity surveyors often re-measure what the model already knows. GenQ reads the quantities straight from Revit and prices them.

What it does

Tick the element types to price and give each one a unit, a division and section code (CSI MasterFormat 2018 or a custom list), a rate and a description. GenQ measures the model and exports the bill of quantities to Excel, PDF or CSV from a docked panel, so you keep working in Revit while it is open.

Version 3 measures the model in one pass instead of one per type, adds phase, design option and level filters, counts repeated placements of linked models, and matches categories by Revit's built-in ids, so it works in any Revit language. Units cover count, length, area, volume, weight from material density, and one line per material layer. Settings and rates are saved in the model itself, and price lists move between projects as Excel files.

Every third-party library is bundled into one assembly and loaded in a private context, so GenQ never clashes with Revit's own copies or with other add-ins.

HighlightSelf-tested inside Revit 2024, 2025 and 2026 on Autodesk's Snowdon Towers sample: 392 types read in 1.7 to 3.4 s, 136 BOQ lines calculated in under a second, and the same grand total in every version.
